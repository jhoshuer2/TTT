Questions from partners

Good to know.

We’ll help find the right format for your audience and goals.

Who can explore a partnership?

Software testing and QA tools, SaaS companies, technology brands, service providers and other organisations with something relevant to the quality community.

What information is in the partnership deck?

It’s the best place to get current audience details, event dates, available partnership formats, newsletter specifications and next steps.

Can we shape a custom programme?

Yes. Tell us what you want to share and who you hope to reach. We’ll explore a format that fits the community and your goals.

How soon should we get in touch?

For conferences and co-branded programmes, earlier is better so there is time to plan the audience, format and approvals together.

Can we use The Test Tribe’s logo or co-brand an event?

Logo use and co-branded materials need review and approval. We’ll share the right guidelines as part of the planning conversation.
